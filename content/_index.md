I am a postdoc at Ruhr-Universität Bochum, working in the research group Algebraic Combinatorics led by [Christian Stump](https://homepage.rub.de/christian.stump/). My position is funded through the project “[Machine learning combinatorial statistics and maps](https://combinatorial-synergies.de/projects/?elem=Machine-learning-combinatorial-statistics-and-maps)” within the DFG SPP Combinatorial Synergies program.

I received my PhD in June 2025 from KTH Royal Institute of Technology under the supervision of [Katharina Jochemko](https://www.kth.se/profile/jochemko) and [Petter Brändén](https://www.kth.se/profile/pbranden/). My thesis is available here: [Enumerative and matroidal aspects of rook placements](https://kth.diva-portal.org/smash/record.jsf?pid=diva2%3A1958137&dswid=6895).

## Research {#research}

My research is in algebraic and geometric combinatorics, and their interface with the geometry of polynomials. Specifically, I am interested in the connections between matroid theory, discrete geometry, and log-concave phenomena in combinatorics.
